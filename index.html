XC40 Laadmonitor - Terms and Conditions

XC40 Laadmonitor is a personal, non-commercial application for monitoring the battery and charging status of the owner's Volvo XC40.

The application is intended exclusively for use by its owner and is not offered to third parties.

Vehicle data is used solely to support personal home charging decisions based on electricity prices and solar energy availability.
